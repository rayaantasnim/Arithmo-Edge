07 · Zero-Fragmentation Olympiad Tag Policy

Tags Locked in Rated Rounds · Unlocked in Practice

In authentic Olympiads like the IMO or Putnam, problems never arrive pre-labeled with the theorem required to solve them. Sub-topics such as #Modular-Arithmetic, #Vieta-Jumping, #Inversion, and #Pigeonhole are strictly hidden during live rated contests and unlocked exclusively during unrated practice and Arithmo Database review.

Exposing lemma names prematurely collapses genuine problem-solving intuition into mechanical formula recall. By withholding sub-topic metadata at both the UI layer and Cloudflare Worker edge payload via Anti-Spoiler Sub-Topic Masking EngineTagArchitect (Zero-Fragmentation Shield)Strips lemma hints such as #LTE, #Inversion, or #Vieta-Jumping from live contest payloads so solvers diagnose structural invariants authentically until rounds conclude., every solver learns to diagnose structural invariants blindly under realistic championship timing.

Once concluded rounds graduate into the public archive (except suspended <3 quorum sets), full theorem indexing automatically opens for Subsite #13 filtering and V10.3 Smart Mistake Deck diagnostics via WeaknessTargetedGen AI.
